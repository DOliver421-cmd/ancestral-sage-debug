## PRICING VALUE ANALYSIS

### Free ($0)
**Value:** Discovery + basic community access
**Retention hook:** See what's possible, want more
**Conversion trigger:** "I want to create/post/chat"

### Member ($9.99/mo)
**Value:** Community participation + learning
**Retention hook:** Course progress + social connections
**Conversion trigger:** "I want to create and earn"

### Plus ($19.99/mo)
**Value:** Creator tools + publishing
**Retention hook:** First sales + content creation
**Conversion trigger:** "I want advanced analytics + team"

### Pro ($39.99/mo)
**Value:** Professional tools + business features
**Retention hook:** Revenue growth + efficiency
**Conversion trigger:** "I need automation + scale"

### Studio ($59.99/mo)
**Value:** Business operations + team collaboration
**Retention hook:** Business growth + market expansion
**Conversion trigger:** "I need full platform control"

### Director ($99.99/mo)
**Value:** Full platform + AI orchestration
**Retention hook:** Competitive advantage + innovation
**Conversion trigger:** "I am the platform"

## UPGRADE PATH LOGIC

Each tier answers: "What can I do now that I could not before?"

```
FREE → MEMBER
"I can now create posts, join chat, and access 5 courses"
(Community participation)

MEMBER → PLUS
"I can now create courses, publish content, and earn money"
(Creator economy)

PLUS → PRO
"I can now use advanced AI, analytics, and business tools"
(Professional capabilities)

PRO → STUDIO
"I can now collaborate with teams, automate workflows, and scale"
(Business operations)

STUDIO → DIRECTOR
"I can now orchestrate the entire platform with AI"
(Full platform control)
```
