🚫 Red Flags (Don't Do These)

  • ❌ Writing full sentences ("I want a happy song about cats")
  • ❌ Using artist names (Suno ignores or blocks them)
  • ❌ Being vague ("make it sound good")
  • ❌ Forgetting the [End] tag (songs cut off mid-word)
  • ❌ Extending from the very last second (audio melts)
