CLUSTER 1: POLICING & SCHOOLS (Tactics 1–10)

TACTIC 1

Racial Profiling

Checklist: Distribute KYR cards; hold stop scenario role‑plays; recruit legal observers; record/report incidents immediately.

Curriculum: Tier 1 — KYR: Stops & Searches. Tier 2 — Role‑play trainer pack. Tier 3 — Oversee reporting workflow.

Policy: Require bodycam + data release; independent stop review boards; racial‑profiling reporting mandates.

Extra: Incident log template, KPIs = # reported stops, resolution time.
