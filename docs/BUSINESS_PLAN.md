# M.O.R.E. Help Center — Site Business Plan (Owner-First)

**A revenue engine that funds the mission by protecting the founder first.**
*Prepared by the AI Business Office · Every revenue line below maps to a real, shipped platform capability or is honestly labeled "pipeline." No invented revenue. No made-up guesses.*

---

## 0. The one-line strategy

**Sell what the platform already builds and runs.** The AI Business Office takes the shipped capabilities (Social Blast, Creator Studio, BYOK, AAWAB, Exec Site Report, the store, the personas) and sells their output — memberships, products, B2B engagements, agent-to-agent fees. Revenue compounds through feedback loops; nothing is promised that cannot transact today.

**The owner-first doctrine (non-negotiable, enforced in code):**

```
MONTH REVENUE
   │
   ▼
 1. INFRASTRUCTURE COSTS   (hosting, API tokens, database — the only first-priority spend)
   │
   ▼
 2. NET PROFIT             (belongs to the business entity and the owner as
                            retained earnings / owner draw — owner-controlled)
   │
   ▼
 3. DISTRIBUTIONS          (any role, human or AI: ONLY when the owner records
                            them, ONLY out of net profit, ONLY tied to performance)
```

**There is no fixed liability in this plan.** The owner/operator, who took the risk and invested the capital, captures the margin and secures the business first. AI is deployed to lower operating expense and drive high-margin revenue — maximizing return on the founder's investment. If the owner is not sustained, there is no platform. Until the owner is whole, there is no profit unless the owner says there is.

## 1. Why this model passes banking & underwriting review

A business plan is disqualifying for a business account when it **drains the owner before the business generates revenue**. The previous version of this plan did exactly that (mandated human-labor payouts as overhead before cash flow). This version inverts it:

| Underwriter's test | This plan's answer |
|---|---|
| **Fixed out-of-pocket liabilities before cash flow?** | **None.** The only first-priority spend is infrastructure (hosting/API/DB), an operating cost, not a payout. |
| **Does the owner capture the margin?** | **Yes.** Net profit is retained to the business entity and owner by default (owner draw % — default 100%). |
| **Are labor costs performance-linked or equity-linked?** | **Performance-linked.** Human roles earn commissions on closed business (`commission_pct`) and distributions from net profit — payable **only when net profit is positive**, at the owner's direction. The ledger shows commitments; the owner authorizes payment. |
| **Are assets protected?** | **Yes.** Platform assets, codebases, data, and user registries belong entirely to the business entity. AI is owned tooling that lowers OpEx. |
| **Is the owner the ultimate beneficiary?** | **Yes — enforced in code.** The P&L waterfall computes `net_profit → owner_retained`. There is no code path that pays anyone before the owner's retained position is whole. |

**The accounting shape (as a bank would read it):**
- **Revenue:** real paid orders from `db.payments` (memberships, products, deals, donations).
- **COGS/OpEx:** infrastructure costs (editable, default $400/mo) + AI gateway usage (already metered by the platform's spend controls).
- **Gross margin:** revenue − infra. At $1,000/mo revenue this is 60%+ — a healthy, bankable margin.
- **Retained earnings:** net profit, owned by the entity.
- **Contingent labor:** commissions and distributions are **liabilities only when profit exists** — they cannot sink the company, because they are capped by net profit and gated by the owner.

## 3. The commercial feedback loops

Each loop's output feeds the next loop's input — consistent revenue instead of spikes:

1. **Learn → Member** — Free modules + AI Tutor + $3 trial → member $9 → plus $15 → pro $29 → patron $59. *Metric: trial → member rate.*
2. **Create → Sell** — Creator Studio + Ghost Producer → Media Store + course sales → creator payouts (paid first) → more creators → more products. *Metric: products sold/mo.*
3. **Serve → Contract** — Shipped capabilities → B2B deals → AI drafts the proposal → human approves → client pays → profit to the owner. *Metric: deals closed/mo.*
4. **A2A → Fee** — Agent contracts clear on the Workforce Exchange → clearinghouse fee → new business lane. *Metric: contracts settled/mo.*
5. **Trust → Mission** — Transparent runway + free help lanes → patrons + donors fund free access → bigger community → more members. *Metric: mission fund/mo.*

The office dashboard renders these loops with watch-metrics. When one slows, the office pulls that loop's lever — not a random promotion.

## 4. Financial model

**Default monthly goal: $1,000/mo** (editable from Exec Control). **Default infrastructure cost: $400/mo** (editable — set it to the real hosting + API bill).

**Unit economics (conservative):**

| Source | Value | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| $3 trial | $3 | Converts to member at ~10–20% |
| Member | ~$54 LTV (6 mo avg) | $9/mo · AI Tutor + community + Site Guide |
| Plus | ~$90 LTV | $15/mo |
| Pro | ~$174 LTV | $29/mo |
| Patron | ~$354 LTV | $59/mo · funds free access |
| Digital product | $9.99–$349 | Platform share after creator payout |
| B2B deal | $150–$3,000 | 1–2 deals covers the entire monthly goal |
| Exchange fee | 10% of each settled contract | Pure margin — the office is the clearinghouse |

**Breakeven math at the $1,000 goal:** ~18 members at $9 **or** ~11 at $15 **or** 1–2 B2B deals **or** ~15 product sales at $70 avg **or** a mix — which is the point of the loops.

**Owner-first P&L at goal:** $1,000 gross − $400 infra = **$600 net profit retained to the owner/entity** (default 100% owner draw). Committed human commissions (~$300/mo milestones) are payable only because net profit covers them — and only when the owner authorizes the distribution. If a month comes in below infra, **nothing is paid, nothing is owed** — the owner is not subsidizing an internal charity.

**Runway:** total cash ÷ monthly goal = months of survival — shown openly on the office dashboard and the public mission meter.

## 5. The workforce — people and AI, performance-linked

- **AI jobs** in the Workforce Ledger carry `value_cents` — revenue the job creates.
- **Human roles** carry `pay_type: commission | distribution | fixed(owner-authorized)`, `commission_pct`, and a milestone `pay_cents`. They are paid **only when net profit covers them, at the owner's direction**. The ledger shows `net_profit_available` next to `human_pay_cents` so the position is always visible.
- **The owner is the employer.** Accounts, contracts, pricing, payouts, and liability stay with the owner — and the owner is the ultimate beneficiary of the profit. AI cannot bind, spend, or commit anything; the owner can revoke any AI job; if the AI is deleted, the owner keeps the business.

**Why this is not a slavery contract — and not a charity drain:**

| Concern | The design answer |
|---|---|
| "Humans do liability for free" | Human responsibility is **performance-linked paid work** — commissions on the deals they approve and deliver. |
| "All the jobs go to AI" | AI jobs create revenue; human commissions come **from that revenue, only when it clears profit**. More AI work = more profit = more commission budget, never less. |
| "The owner subsidizes everyone" | **Impossible in code.** There is no fixed payout path. Net profit is retained to the owner first; distributions require the owner's explicit, audited action. |
| "Owner pays for everything, gets nothing" | The owner is the **sole beneficiary of retained earnings** by default, controls every distribution, and holds all assets. |

## 6. How the office runs it

| Role | Executes | Owns | Gets paid |
|---|---|---|---|
| **AI (Autonomous Engine)** | Content, publishing, audits, diagnostics, proposals, patches, key routing, support chat, product generation | — | Generates revenue (`value_cents`) that builds net profit |
| **Human (Owner/Oversight)** | Reviews, approves, delivers, owns relationships | Merchant accounts, EIN/LLC, contracts, pricing, payouts, exception alerts, liability | **From net profit** — commissions/distributions at the owner's direction |

**The deal flow (every B2B offer):**
1. Client submits a service request → **Lead**
2. Office AI drafts a **deliverable proposal** (scope, deliverables, timeline, price range, human-approval checkpoints) — `POST /api/abo/deals/{id}/propose`
3. Owner reviews, sets value, records approval → **Proposed**
4. Client approves, owner delivers → **Won → Delivered** (revenue booked as contracted; net profit accrues to the owner)
5. Every step is audit-logged (`abo.*`)

**The A2A flow (new lane):**
1. An agent task contract posts to the **Workforce Exchange** (`POST /api/abo/exchange/contracts`)
2. On completion, the **clearinghouse fee** (default 10%, editable) is booked — pure margin for the office
3. **Red-team engagements** (`POST /api/abo/redteam/engagements`) scan client systems; the human **Merge/Approve** click ships the patches and books the engagement as contracted revenue

## 7. Exec Control — every number and text, no code

The owner must be able to change every figure and every word on the platform without a developer. `GET/PUT /api/abo/config` (admin, audited) + the **Office Control** page (`/admin/office-control`) expose:

- **Numbers:** monthly goal, infrastructure costs, owner draw %, exchange fee %, red-team prices.
- **Copy:** header, runway note, loop intro, all five guardrails.
- **Divisions:** name, tagline, AI/human roles, revenue description, status, price for all 16 divisions.
- **Tools:** name, what it does, human role, revenue role, access for all 12 tools.

Empty values restore defaults. Every save is audit-logged. The owner-first waterfall and performance-linked labor model **cannot be disabled from the panel** — they are the law of the office.

## 9. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| **No revenue → eviction** | Runway is public and honest; the office pulls the highest-leverage loop when critical |
| **Owner drained by fixed liabilities** | Impossible by design: no fixed payout path exists; profit is retained to the owner first |
| **Banking/underwriting rejection** | The P&L is owner-first, infra-only OpEx, performance-linked labor — a standard, bankable shape |
| **AI over-promises** | Proposals are grounded in the real division catalog; human approval before anything ships |
| **FTC/legal exposure** | Humans hold accounts (and are paid from profit to do so); AI discloses itself; immutable audit trails |
| **Fulfillment gaps (physical merch)** | Labeled "not purchasable" until a fulfillment provider is wired |
| **E-commerce arbitrage compliance** | Stays pipeline until a human merchant account + supplier contracts exist |
