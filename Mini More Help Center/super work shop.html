📋 The "Saaaay Less" Cheat Sheet

Print this. Tattoo it on your forearm. Use it every day.

🧠 The #1 Rule

❌ "Write me a song about love"

✅ "You are a Nashville songwriter. Write 3 chorus hooks for a country breakup song about a truck driver who just found out his ex is dating his brother. Make it funny AND sad. Give me 3 options in a numbered list."

📝 The 5-Part Formula (Memorize This)

[ROLE] + [TASK] + [CONTEXT] + [TONE] + [FORMAT] "You are a [JOB]. Write me [WHAT]. It's about [WHO/WHAT]. Make it [MOOD]. Give me [HOW IT LOOKS]."

🔄 The "Make It More..." Rewrite Tricks

"Make it shorter"
"Make it funnier"
"Make it sound like Beyoncé"
"Make it more dramatic"
"Rewrite but keep the same idea"
"Give me 3 more options but different"
"Now make it for a different audience"
"Simplify this like I'm 5 years old"

🚫 The 5 Things That Make AI Give You Garbage

  • ❌ Being vague: "Write something good"
  • ❌ Asking for everything at once: "Write my whole album"
  • ❌ Not telling it WHO to be: "Write a song" (be WHO?)
  • ❌ Accepting the first answer: It can do BETTER. Push it.
  • ❌ Not saving your good prompts: You'll forget what worked.
